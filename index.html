Honest questions

Why not just use Excel or Google Sheets?

They can deduplicate and trim, but they silently change values (leading zeros, dates), do not detect unbalanced quotes or encoding damage, and never tell you which rows were invalid. TableDoctor reports exactly what it changed.

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment, the file is yours. There is no server to keep running, so there is nothing to charge you for monthly.

Can it read my .xlsx?

Yes, the first sheet, including cells stored as text. Formulas are read as their last displayed value by the bundled SheetJS library.

Do you guarantee my CRM import will succeed?

No. It removes the usual data-level causes of failure. Field mapping and CRM-side validation rules remain yours to check — that is why the report lists every change.

What do you do with my data?

Nothing: it never reaches us. There is no server, no analytics script and no third-party request after the page loads. You can verify it in the network tab.
